Questions, answered.

Short answers to what people ask most.

What is Forward Deployed AI Agent Engineering?

It combines forward deployed engineering with AI agent engineering. You learn to understand business problems, build AI solutions, connect them to enterprise systems and take them to production.

Will I learn to build AI agents?

Yes. You cover tool calling, memory, LangGraph, human approval, MCP, multi-agent patterns and common failure modes.

Which tools will I use?

Python, FastAPI, SQL, Docker, Terraform, AWS, Azure and GCP, LangGraph, Make and n8n, vector databases, dbt, Airflow and modern LLMs.

Is there interview preparation?

Yes. It covers case-style discovery rounds, live coding and integrations, AI system design and behavioural questions.

What is the capstone?

An end-to-end deployment that brings together discovery, integration, AI engineering, deployment, evaluation and production readiness.
